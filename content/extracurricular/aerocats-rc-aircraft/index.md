As Manufacturing & Testing Engineer with AeroCats, UC's RC Aircraft Design & Manufacturing Club, I helped build four RC aircraft with 16-foot wingspans for national intercollegiate competitions, laser-cutting more than a thousand pieces of hardwood, pine, and balsa and assembling them by hand. I also designed thrust tests for six turbine models to figure out which engine would carry the most payload. The team finished **4th overall in the Regular Class at SAE Aero Design West**, May 2023.
