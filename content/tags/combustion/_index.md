---
title: 'Combustion'
---

In a high-speed combustor the air moves too fast for a flame to survive on its own, so a bluff body parks a slow, hot pocket where the flame can anchor. I study how spray, mixing and heat release behave in that anchoring region, and what drives the flame to oscillate.

## Publications

- K. Manu, N. Truong, P. Khare. *Large Eddy Simulations of Spray and Combustion Processes in a Bluff Body Stabilized High-Speed Combustor*. Manuscript in preparation, targeting *Applications in Energy and Combustion Science*.
