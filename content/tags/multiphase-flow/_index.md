---
title: 'Multiphase Flow'
---

Liquids rarely stay whole in a fast flow. A fuel droplet behind a shock, or a gel jet leaving an injector, comes apart into a spray, and the size and timing of that breakup decide how well a propellant burns. I study how the breakup happens. What sets the droplet sizes, and how fast the liquid surface tears apart.
