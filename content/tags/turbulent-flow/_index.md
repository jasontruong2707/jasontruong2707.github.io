---
title: 'Turbulent Flow'
---

Most combustors stay lit because a pocket of recirculating flow keeps feeding heat back to fresh reactants. I study the turbulent structure of those flows. Where the recirculation sits, how it oscillates, and which large-scale motions carry the most energy.

## Publications

- N. Truong, K. Manu. *Large Eddy Simulations of Recirculation Structures in Isothermal Swirling Coaxial Jet*. Manuscript in preparation, targeting *Physics of Fluids*.
